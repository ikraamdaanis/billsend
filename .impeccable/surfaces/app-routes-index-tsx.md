# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the marketing homepage (`app/routes/index.tsx`). Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: freelancers and solo operators who need to bill a client now and are wary of giving client and bank details to a website. They should leave believing billsend is quick and genuinely private, and start an invoice.

Proof on hand: the live request tally, the Content-Security-Policy, the public source, the absence of any sign-up, faithful sample invoices. No testimonials or usage numbers exist.

Constraints: privacy copy must stay literally true (files the user saves are the only way data leaves); the help channel is GitHub only; the editor's visual world is untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a security-printed document. billsend's privacy is presented the way a banknote presents its anti-forgery features: plain verbs a visitor checks in seconds on any device. It refuses the headline-plus-screenshot-plus-feature-cards template and the serif-on-cream editorial look the page wore before.

OWN-WORLD: Pale mint-grey security tint as the ground, with a fine guilloche underprint generated in SVG; intaglio ink in billsend blue for type; numbering-machine red only for serials and live counters; microprint rules and perforations as the only dividers; white paper slips for invoices. One full-bleed intaglio-blue band carries the privacy checks. Archivo at 125% width for engraved headlines and field labels, Archivo at 62% width for numerals, Geist for reading text. Controls are rectangular engraved plates with a hairline inner frame, 4px corners.

STORY: The visitor reads "Send the invoice. Keep everything else.", types a client's name on the specimen invoice, watches the red counter of requests to other websites stay at 000, checks four plain privacy features, sees what a full invoice carries, clears their doubts in grouped questions, and creates an invoice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A guilloche-bordered document fills the content width. Full width: the wide-caps headline on two lines, the second in brand blue. Below it, left 6/11: an 18px lead; the "Create invoice" plate and a link to the privacy checks. Right 5/11: a white specimen invoice slip with serial No. 0043 (the same job as the tour invoice), a ruled BILL TO line the visitor can type on, a typeface switch, an amount box, and the red numbering-machine counter of requests to other websites. A transit-symbol line of four true facts runs along the document foot.

FORM: Security-printed cheque and banknote, candidate 4 of 7 on the grounded list. Seed key 7922b6de. Raises: from the single-hue program, the privacy band floods its region in intaglio blue rather than using blue as an accent; from the jacquard, every guilloche is generated deterministically from real parameters on the device; from the exposure record, tints come from one stepped ramp only; from the six-pack, the checks cross-reference each other in one reading order.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
